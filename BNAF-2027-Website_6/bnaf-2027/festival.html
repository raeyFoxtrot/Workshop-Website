From your first glider to your next breakthrough, this is where the love of flight brings us together.

Organised by the Aeromodelling Club, IIT Kanpur, the festival brings student builders and flyers from across the country together for competition and hands-on learning. Whether you have flown for years or never launched a glider, there is a place for you here.
