Take flight.

The focus of the workshop would be to introduce you to this event and the exciting field of aeromodelling and system engineering. The workshop would offer introductory lectures from Professors, hands-on experience of building an aircraft model and flying them.

What to expect from the workshop at IIT Kanpur

  • Understand the event

    These workshops would introduce you to the event, its structure, the learning experience that one should expect to get and details about participating in the event. Since the event would be conducted in an online mode, students must have a clear idea about the structure and overall scope of the event.

  • No prior experience required

    The purpose of the workshop is to give an exposure of aeromodelling to students from various streams & backgrounds. No prior experience is required for the workshop.

  • Explore the competition and RC Glider design

    It would introduce you to the details of the problem statement for the competition. It would also contain a detailed presentation about the design of a RC Glider.

  • Learn aircraft design and modelling

    The workshop would be very crucial for students who want to explore and learn about designing and modelling of modern aircrafts. Significant learning experience shall be expected from the workshop.

These workshops would provide you with a networking session with aeromodelling enthusiasts across the country. We hope to see all aero enthusiast students in large numbers!
